What is
Aningalan?

Known as the "Little Baguio of Antique," Aningalan is a misty highland sanctuary sitting 1,000 meters above the tropical heat.

Located in San Remigio, it serves as the province's summer capital, offering pine-scented air, rolling hills, and a unique cool climate rare in the Visayas region.

Core Attractions

🍓 Strawberry Garden Pick fresh berries from Nov–April.
🪨 Igbaclag Cave A natural limestone "stone castle."
🚣 Danao Lake Serene bamboo rafting & picnics.
🌸 Rafflesia Watching Home to the world's largest flower.
